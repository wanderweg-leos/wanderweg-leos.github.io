The puppies are really starting to move around now and are beginning to play a little with each other. Well play might not be quite the right word, but they are now starting to chew on each and are beginning to realize that there are other puppies in the box with them. They are also getting really cuddly when you pick them up and are starting to snuggle! Too cute!
