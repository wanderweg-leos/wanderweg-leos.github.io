Zoey is still feeling great and even got out for a short walk today. She hasn’t started anything yet, but you can feel the little puppies in her tummy moving around. How great is that. They are rolling around and kicking their little feet. It was a beautiful day today and we took these pictures of Zoey and her best friend.
