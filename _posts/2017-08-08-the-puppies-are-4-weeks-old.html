The puppies have had a great week. They are turning into ‘real’ puppies now and are developing their own unique characters! They are playing, running exploring and getting lots of hugs and kisses. They still sleep quite a bit, as it is so hard to grow so fast and learn so many new things. They are getting used to getting brushed and cleaned and their toe nails done. They got to go outside for the first time and did great! They loved the outdoors and the extra space! Some of the puppies explored the puppy ramp and the puppy house, others just hung out on the grass. They also got to play with some of our other dogs, which is always fun to see. They will be growing and exploring a lot this week and are getting super cute. Next week they will even look more like ‘real’ puppies! Though they have had some visitors, they will start to have more and more people come and see them over the next few weeks to help with socializing them to all sorts of different people!
