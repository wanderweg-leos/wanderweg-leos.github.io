Wow how did that happen two weeks old already. The puppies are fantastic - so cute. They are all doing great and are growing, have fat little tummies and are very happy. They are starting to play now and their eyes are open. They are still not liking the heat we are having. We put a few water bottles in the box with cool water and they are sleeping on them - kinda like a little puppy water bed!

Here are their two week photos. They are from top to bottom pink girl, blue boy, green boy and purple boy. Enjoy!
