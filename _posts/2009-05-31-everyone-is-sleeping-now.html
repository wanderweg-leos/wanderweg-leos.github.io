After some fun play time the puppies one by one get tired and take a little cat nap - but my little helper isn't quite done with the fun play time yet. She tries to get the puppies up and moving, but when puppies are tired and happy they just go to sleep anyway. But they only sleep for a little while and are then ready to play again!
