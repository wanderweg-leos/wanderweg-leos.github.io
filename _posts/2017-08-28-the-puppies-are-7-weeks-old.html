The puppies had an awesome week. They had lots of visitors and enjoyed the company of all the people they saw. They got to play with the other dogs and love to grab onto their tails. They have also found their teeth and have great chewing power now! They will learn soon that biting is not that fun! They are the best puppies in the morning and are very peaceful and patient for us to wake up and make them their breakfast. The most patient puppies ever! Which is very nice for us. They will have a big week this week and will get vet checked and vaccinated and start getting ready for their new homes!
