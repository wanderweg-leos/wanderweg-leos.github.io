After the puppies had finished their lunch, my little helper thought they might need some more. Pinky was having a little nap while the other pups were still hanging out near the food bowls. So the events that took place next are as follows:

Photo 1 – I’ll take that please, pinky might need some more dinner. Photo 2 – Here I come pinky. Photo 3 – Here you go pinky have some yummy food. Photo 4 – Okay then, I’ll show you how to get the food from the bowl. Photo 5 – Well if that doesn’t work I guess I’ll just have to feed you one piece of kibble at a time!

Too cute!
