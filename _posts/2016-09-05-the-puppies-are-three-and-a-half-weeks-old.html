Wow what a big week the puppies have had. The puppies have moved out of their whelping box and into a new puppy room. They are very happy with their new space. They are now drinking formula and today had their first bit of canned puppy food. They are growing and growing and are so cute! The puppies are very messy drinkers and get formula everywhere! We have had some puppy visitors and the puppies are very social, they have also met most of the other dogs. Pixie is being a fantastic aunt to them and stays with them whenever she is allowed! The puppies also are getting their teeth, with the sharp canines are coming through. They will continue to get more visitors through the next few weeks to help with their socialization.
