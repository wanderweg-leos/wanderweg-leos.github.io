At 21 days of age the puppies are ready for more space and want out of their box! It’s like clockwork with these puppies. So at 3 weeks of age, Pixie’s puppies moved into the puppy room where they can move around more. The box is still in the room for a few more days so they can sleep in their at night and the couch will stay for a few more days too until they need more room to romp around! The puppies are doing great and are getting so cute. They can see and hear well now and are learning to move and play. They are also getting good at giving kisses.
