Here is a picture of Zoey taken today! The weather was finally sunny here in Nova Scotia and the dogs were all too hot! They enjoyed the day hanging out in their new big yard! They are not used to the warmer days as we have had so few this summer. You can see that Zoey’s tummy is getting pretty big! One and a half weeks left until the puppies arrive. Zoey is a beautiful Leonberger, she is so pretty and so sweet. We love her!
