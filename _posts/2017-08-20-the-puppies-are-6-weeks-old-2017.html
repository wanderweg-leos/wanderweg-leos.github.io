The puppies are keeping us so very busy it's hard to find the time to do anything even answer the phone, get on email or post to the blog. They are a wonderful bunch of puppies. We love the so much! They have been getting lots of visitors and are super social with everyone. They are loving their puppy yard and love the play station that they have. They also just love to play with each other. They are just starting to develop their own little personalities. Some are busy, some are quieter, some are more vocal... one is very vocal... but they are all wonderful and so sweet.
