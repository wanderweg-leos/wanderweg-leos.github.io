What could be better than a pile of sweet puppies sitting on your lap? The puppies are getting quite interactive now and will give you kisses, will play with their toys and will crawl into your lap for some snuggle time! Their sharp little teeth are really coming in now and their little mouths are starting to think it is quite interesting to chew on things. Puppies will be puppies and they do love to chew!
