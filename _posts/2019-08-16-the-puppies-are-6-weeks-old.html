The puppies are doing great and are growing like bad weeds. They are running everywhere and getting into whatever they can. They love to go into any door that gets opened. The closets are the best places to investigate. The puppies have been playing with some of the older dogs and have been loving their toy mobile. They love to play with the hanging items! The puppies have also been having visitors each day and are very social and friendly puppies! They also get a new stuffed toy each day that is a different size and texture. It’s so hard to believe they are already six weeks old!
