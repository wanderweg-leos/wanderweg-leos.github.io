Stella is just the sweetest dog you can ever imagine. She loves to sit on the couch with the kids and snuggle up close and her best buddy equally loves to snuggle up with Stella. Stella always has to be with the kids and the kids will have it no other way! They are always asking ‘can Stella come with us’ no matter where we are going or what we are doing! We love you Stella! Too cute!
