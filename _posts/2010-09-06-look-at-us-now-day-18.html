The pups are really changing now and are walking and playing and even giving a little bark or two! They are starting to chew on each other and are just TOO cute. You can feel little bumps where their teeth are coming so soon they will have little sharps coming through their gums. Still will be a few weeks until they have enough pressure in their jaws for any biting action. The pups are growing and growing and weigh around 3 – 3.5 lbs! The photos are from top to bottom pink girl, blue boy, green boy and purple boy. Too cute!
