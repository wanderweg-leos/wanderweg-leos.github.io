We have had a busy few days here at Wanderweg Kennel. The pups continue to grow and grow. Their inside area has been expanded to the entire puppy room and the whelping box has been removed. We have great flooring for the pups that help with their footing which is so important for young puppies. The puppies have lots of toys but love the kids the most. They are still pretty gentle with them so we are hoping we have a few more days before the puppies turn into chewing machines.
