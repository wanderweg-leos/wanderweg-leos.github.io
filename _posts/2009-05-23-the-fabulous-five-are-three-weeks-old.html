The puppies celebrated their three week birthday yesterday! Wow three weeks have already gone by – seems like yesterday they arrived. We always give a little sigh of relief when the puppies reach the three week mark as the first three weeks are the most stressful when you have puppies. So far, these sweet puppies have been easy, for which we are very grateful, but it isn’t always that way.

My little helper had much fun helping with the pictures today. She is really enjoying the puppies and is going to be quite sad when they start chewing on her legs, toes and fingers! Yikes, the puppies are starting to get their teeth! You can see little white nibs poking out, but they still do not have any strength when they nibble on you. We still have a week or so of gentleness before they all turn into little chewing machines! The puppy pictures are from the top yellow girl, pink girl, red girl, green boy and bottom is purple boy!
