Wow the puppies are already 8 weeks old. They are all such lovely puppies! The puppies are getting ready to go to their new homes! We are going to miss them so much. The puppies have been vet checked, microchipped and vaccinated and all did amazing during their puppy checkups. They are very active puppies and love to play in the water bowl, play with their mobile, but mostly they love to play with each other and us too. They are adorable. Here they are at 8 weeks of age!

Midnight Blue Girl – still the smallest but has a big personality- we love her!

Dark Pink Girl – lots of energy this little girl has – we love her!

Goldie Girl – she love to give kisses – such a sweetie!

Light Pink Girl – just a sweetie too – so cute!

Purple Girl – one of the darker puppies and with the sweeties face!

Red Girl – the quietest of the bunch but like all the puppies has some energy!

Teal Girl – we love this little girl, she is going to be just like mom Tia!

Yellow Girl – she is growing fast – and is quite adorable!

Lime Girl – a beautiful Leo puppy – picture perfect!

Black Boy – one laid back boy he is! So cute!

Green Boy – has a mind of his own this Leo boy does! Adorable!
