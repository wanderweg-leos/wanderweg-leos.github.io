Just like his big daddy Jefferson, young Rangeley (Wanderweg’s Feature Story) has started to do therapy work at hospitals and nursing homes. Rangeley is a big sweet young pup (11 months in the photo) and everyone who sees him loves him and he is able to bring lots of smiles to lots of faces. What a great way to spend the day, bringing smiles to faces! We are very proud of both Rangeley and Jefferson for participating in such an important activity.
