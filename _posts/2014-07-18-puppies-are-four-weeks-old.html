The puppies had a busy week. They moved out of their box at 3 weeks of age and into the full room. They loved the bigger space at first, but now after a week have already outgrown the room. They will be moving into the family room over the weekend. They have their teeth now and can walk and run and play. They are very social puppies! They have been meeting our other dogs and spending some time in the kitchen. They are all doing great and are as cute as can be.
