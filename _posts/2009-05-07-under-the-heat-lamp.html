Warmth is really important to baby puppies, but keeping the room too warm is really hard on mom, so we use a heat lamp over the whelping box. While mom is outside the box you can see that the puppies all snuggle together under the heat lamp where they are nice and toasty! Mom still spends most of her time with them and is quite happy just hanging out with her puppies. They are too cute!
