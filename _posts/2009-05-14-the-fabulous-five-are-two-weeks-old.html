The fabulous five turned 2 weeks old today and they had a busy day! They all got weighed and then had their first deworming meds. They weigh between 3 1/2 and 5 lbs. Yes, the purple boy is the pup that weighs 5 lbs! He is one big boy. The pups also had their first set of individual photos! They are really starting to move around now and can almost walk, though they look like drunken sailors and still tip over easily. They are starting to play and you can see that pink girl is playing with the toy! Yellow and red girl's eyes are pretty well open, but they still cannot see very well. The puppies are all doing very well and are too cute!
