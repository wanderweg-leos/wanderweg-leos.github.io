Wow where does the time go, it’s hard to believe these chunky monkey puppies were born 2 weeks ago. Talia’s puppies are all doing awesome and Talia is being the perfect mom. She is totally calm with her puppies. The puppies are now trying to walk around their box and their eyes are somewhat open so they can see some light and shadows. They are very snuggle and love to be held and snuggled. A few of the largest puppies are now over 3 pounds! This is a big week for them, by the end of this week they will be able to see, walk and hear. Lots of growing to do this week!
