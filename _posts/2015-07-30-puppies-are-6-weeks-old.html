Wow where do the time go… the puppies are now over 6 weeks old. They are such great puppies, very sweet and friendly, though they are finding their sharp little pin teeth! They are starting to bite on your pant legs and shake their little heads! That’s such fun. The weather hasn’t been great but they are spending some time outside when the sun is out (which sadly isn’t very often this summer!).
