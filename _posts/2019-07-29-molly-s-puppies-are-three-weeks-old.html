Molly's puppies are all doing great. They are now three weeks old and are enjoying some new space and freedom! At three weeks they are ready to climb out of the box and start exploring. They also had their first meal of some gruel and loved it! They are all growing well and are very sweet puppies. Hopefully soon they will get to move in with Pixie's puppies. But 5 days is big age difference so it will take a bit of time for them to catch up to the others. Their eyes are now open and they are seeing and hearing and getting very cuddly.
