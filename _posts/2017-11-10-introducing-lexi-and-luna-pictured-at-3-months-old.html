We kept two amazing puppies from our summer 2017 litters. Lexi formally Talia Dark Pink girl is going to grow up just like her mom Talia! We love her to pieces. She is so smart with a great sense of humour and is always getting into mischief! What a cutie she is! Luna formally Ruby Purple girl is not that much like her mom or dad but must be like some relative in her background! She is a solid puppy, a very wide girl and as sweet as can be. She just likes to say hi and get pats and though she gets into mischief too that’s only because she is following the footsteps of her sister Lexi! So much cuteness with these two sweet Leo girls!
