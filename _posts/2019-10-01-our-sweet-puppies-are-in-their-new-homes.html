The puppies have now traveled far and wide and are settled into their new families. We are so lucky to have such great families that open their hearts and homes to our beautiful puppies. Each puppy has its own character and way, but all are equally special. We hope that these lovely puppies have long and happy lives and that they bring great joy to their new families. We hope they are all treasured and loved each and every day. Thank you to our new families for taking such good care of these sweet puppies.
