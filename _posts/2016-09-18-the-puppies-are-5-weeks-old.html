Wow where does the time go! The puppies are doing awesome and are the sweetest bunch of puppies ever! Yesterday they got to go outside for the first time and today they had a blast going in and out! They are starting to enjoy some of their playground equipment which will be introduced over the week! They were very helpful when we put their first play equipment together! The other dogs are very fond of the puppies and enjoy hanging out with them too! All puppies are about the same size, none are small and none are too big and they are all beautiful!
