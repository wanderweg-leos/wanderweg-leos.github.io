My littlest helper had a great time playing with the puppies this afternoon. They are starting to crawl into her lap and she loves it! Every chance she gets she tries to climb into the box and she is very gentle with the puppies. Her smile lights up the room when she is playing with them and they seem to like her company too! The only problem we have is that she isn’t very happy when she has to leave them and gets shut out of the box! Too cute!
