The puppies have transformed into real puppies! They are moving around everywhere, eating some real puppy mush, interacting with the other dogs and learning all about the sounds they might hear. Some of those are doorbell sounds, fireworks and other everyday sounds. They also get to listing to classic music too! They have been introduced to the vacuum which didn't bother them at all! And they get a new and different toy a few times a day. Puppies are doing great and soon will be open for visitors to learn about people too!
