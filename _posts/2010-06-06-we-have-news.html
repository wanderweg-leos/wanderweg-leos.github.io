Well after waiting for what seems like forever Priscilla is finally in season! We are so excited about our upcoming breeding with Priscilla (Wanderweg’s Classic Design). We are so pleased to announce that Priscilla will be bred to the truly spectacular Kwenobe Balfen Hullanta (Lanta). Lanta lives in England so our attempt at puppies will be with frozen. We are so thankful to Lanta’s owners for everything they have done to make this breeding happen. Everyone who follows this blog please send great thoughts for lots of puppies over the next couple of weeks! We will update our news as it happens! Stay-tuned!
