Hard to believe these sweet puppies are already five weeks old! The time goes by too fast. They are just so cute and are doing everything five week old puppies should be doing! They love to be outside and run in the grass. They love to eat the grass and chew on sticks. They love to dig big holes and then eat the dirt. They love to give kisses and then to chew on your shoes or pant legs or whatever they can grab on to. They love to look so sweet. So here they are pink girl, blue boy, green boy and purple boy. Enjoy!
