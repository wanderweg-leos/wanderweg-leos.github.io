The puppies are doing great! The are almost all near or over 4 lbs and are all fat and happy. Their eyes somewhat open and they are starting to hear a little. They are trying to walk too but mostly fall over. It is hot out so they are loving their water beds to stay cool!

This is a big week… their eyes will fully open and they will be able to hear by the end of it and will be starting to play too! They are as cute as can be!
