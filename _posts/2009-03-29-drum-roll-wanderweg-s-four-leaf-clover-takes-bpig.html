We are so excited to share the news that Caoimhe went Best Puppy in Group at the Cobequid Dog Show in Truro NS! What a huge win and Caoimhe is the first Leonberger puppy to win a BPIG in Atlantic Canada! This was just her second show so we have high hopes for her in the show ring! Caoimhe is a pup from Priscilla’s first litter with Jefferson! The official show photo will be posted when we receive it but for now here she is playing in the snow!
