The puppies had some nice play time with another little helper, though he doesn't consider himself little at 5 years old! He has such a fun time playing with the puppies. They are really starting to realize when someone is in the box with them. The puppies eyes are starting to open now, some more than the others, but for red and pink girls you can see their eyes! How beautiful they are. They still cannot hear anything, but that will start to happen soon too. The pups continue to grow and grow and are becoming a little more interactive, though they are still not doing too much except eating, sleeping and moving about a little bit!
