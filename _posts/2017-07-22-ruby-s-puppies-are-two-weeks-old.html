Ruby and her puppies are doing great! They are absolutely adorable puppies and are very sweet and cuddly. The puppies’ eyes are opening and they are starting to hear. They are also walking around the whelping box. The puppies are all good sized, except little Miss Pink girl who is still a sweet little girl, but she is growing too. They also will have a big week and have lots to learn. Being able to see and hear is amazing.
