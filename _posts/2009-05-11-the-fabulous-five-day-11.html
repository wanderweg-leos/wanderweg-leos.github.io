The puppies continue to grow and are now ravenous most of the time! While Priscilla is still enjoying them she is spending more and more time out of the box. The pups are still happiest when she is in there with them, but when mom wants out that is where she goes. The puppies eyes are now tiny little slits so they should be opening soon and the are trying to get up on their feet a little, but can only get up on their front legs. They are too cute!
