The puppies are really starting to get funny now! They are beginning to be able to hear and try to look at you when you enter the room or sit in the box. They can hear now, but don’t actually understand yet what sound is so it is very funny. They are also trying to play with each other and are really moving around. The pups can actually walk pretty good for their age. They are getting more interactive and will nibble your nose when you hold them and they love to be cuddled and of course they are finding their voices now that they can hear and are learning how to communicate with each other. They are very cute! Today was busy so hopefully tomorrow I will be able to get a few better pictures than I did today!
