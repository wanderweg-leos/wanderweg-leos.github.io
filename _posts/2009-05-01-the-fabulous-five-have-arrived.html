The puppies have arrived and mom did a great job with the whelping. The puppies are very big strong puppies weighing between 600 to 700 grams - those are BIG puppies. We have three sweet girls and two beautiful boys. The puppies have color collars on and that is how each puppy will be named, so we have red girl, pink girl, yellow girl, green boy and purple boy! Mom is pretty tired but is resting comfortably with her puppies.

We are so excited about these puppies and want to thank Kinglord's kennel for daddy Mr. Magnus and also our vet clinic Carnegy Animal Hospital for helping make this dream come true! We hope you will enjoy watching the puppies grow over the next nine weeks!
