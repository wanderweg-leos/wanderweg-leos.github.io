Wow it is hard to believe that the fabulous five are already five weeks old! They are now real little puppies and are doing everything that puppies should be doing. They love to be outside, to play together and to play with the kids, to socialize with visitors and of course to sleep and eat! Now when you walk into the puppy yard they all come running to meet you. They also love to dig in the ground, to eat dirt and sticks and grass and leaves and whatever they can find to put in their little mouths. Their little mouths now carry a bit of a punch when they are nibbling on you! They are all very sweet and very beautiful! The puppies from top to bottom are red girl, pink girl, yellow girl, green boy and purple boy.
