Thank you to all our wonderful families who have opened their homes and hearts to provide our beautiful puppies with such wonderful lives. We are so excited and happy that our amazing puppies are now part of the wonderful families who have adopted them. It is so hard to part with these incredible puppies and having such great homes certainly does make it a bit easier… though we still miss the puppies so much when they move to their forever homes. We know they will all get lots of puppy treats, love and happiness through their lives.
