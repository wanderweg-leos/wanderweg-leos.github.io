Pixie’s puppies are all growing very well. They are entering their week of change where their eyes and ears will open and they will be able to see and hear. Then the fun really begins! The puppies are doing everything they are suppose to be doing. They are a bunch happy puppies! They are starting to play with each other, so cute, and are starting to try to walk around their box! At three weeks they will be ready to move out of their box and into the puppy room. Won’t be long! They grow up so quickly.
