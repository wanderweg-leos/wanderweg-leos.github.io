It doesn’t seem possible that the puppies are already one week old. This has been a very trying litter and time has both flown by and stood still all at the same time! We are very delighted to report that all of the puppies have reached the one week milestone, but several of the puppies are still not gaining as well as I would like to see. So things are still a bit of touch and go with these sweet puppies. Tia is being an amazing mom and the puppies are all very happy and content and doing what one week old puppies do. They are starting to move around the box more and are getting good at snuggling. Fingers crossed that they all continue to grow.
